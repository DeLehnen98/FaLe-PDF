Why is FaLe PDF free?

Because almost everything happens on your device – and that costs us nothing.

On-device processing

Reading, signing, scanning, text recognition, converting and editing run right on your phone or computer. No servers needed – so there is nothing to pay for.

Only the AI costs money

The optional cloud AI has real costs per request. That’s why there is a free daily allowance.

No data business

No banner ads, no tracking, no analytics and no selling of data. You only see an ad if you start a video yourself.

Privacy in brief

  • Your files stay on your device. Documents, scans and signatures are stored locally – only what you choose to share is uploaded.
  • No account, no tracking. No sign-up, no analytics or advertising cookies.
  • Cloud AI only with consent. Only the text you select for an AI task is sent; no content is stored.
  • Video ads only if you want them (Android app) – with your consent, in exchange for extra AI requests.

Frequently asked questions

Is FaLe PDF really free?

Yes. All PDF and office features are free – no subscription, no account, no watermarks. Only the optional cloud AI costs money to run: there are free requests every day.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Your files are processed and stored on your device. Only when you use the optional cloud AI is the text you select sent to the AI service – with your consent, and no content is stored. You can share or save files yourself at any time – only then does a file leave your device.

Can I sign PDFs without a subscription?

Yes. Drawing, saving and placing your signature is free and needs no account – just like filling in forms and adding dates, text or stamps.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All tools except the optional cloud AI work without internet – including text recognition (OCR). Its language data (English and German) is downloaded once on first use.

How do I install the app?

Android: the app is coming to Google Play soon. In the browser (Android, computer): open FaLe PDF and choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen” from the browser menu. iPhone/iPad: open it in Safari, tap “Share” and choose “Add to Home Screen”.

Which files can I open?

PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, CSV, TXT, Markdown and images (JPG, PNG, WebP). With “Open with” and “Share” you can take files straight from email, messengers or your file manager.

Are there ads?

No banners and no ads while you work. In the Android app you can choose to watch a video ad to get extra AI requests – that’s all.

Which languages are available?

The app is available in English and German. The letter generators (cancellation, withdrawal etc.) follow German law and create German letters.
