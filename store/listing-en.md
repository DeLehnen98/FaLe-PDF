# Store listing (English) – Google Play

> Google Play limits: title ≤ 30, short description ≤ 80, full description ≤ 4000 characters.
> Do not use other companies' trademarks (no names of other PDF/office apps or vendor product
> names). Naming file formats (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX) is fine.
> The app UI is available in English and German (Profile › Settings › Language; "Automatic" follows
> the device language). Feature names below match the English UI (e.g. "Fill & Sign", "Organize pages",
> "Reading mode", "Redact", "Letters & templates"). The letter generators for cancellations, withdrawals,
> objections etc. follow German law and create German letters – they are labelled "(Germany)" in the app.

## App name (≤ 30)

```
FaLe PDF – PDF & Office
```

Read, sign & scan PDFs – private, offline, with an AI assistant.

## Full description (≤ 4000)

```
FaLe PDF is your private office on your phone: read, fill in, sign, scan and edit PDFs – plus documents, spreadsheets and presentations. Everything runs on your device. No account, no banner ads, no tracking – and AI only when you want it.

READ PDFS
• Fast PDF viewer with search, bookmarks, outline and reading mode
• Read aloud (uses your device's text-to-speech)
• Light and dark mode

FILL & SIGN
• Fill in PDF forms
• Draw, save and place your signature
• Add stamps, dates and initials

ANNOTATE & EDIT
• Highlight, underline, notes and freehand drawing
• Add text and images to PDFs
• Redact: permanently remove confidential content
• Watermarks, page numbers, headers and footers

PAGES & FILES
• Merge PDFs, reorder, rotate, delete, extract and crop pages
• Compress PDFs
• Export pages as images or text
• Add and remove password protection

SCAN
• Scan documents with your camera – with cropping and contrast enhancement
• Scan ID cards (front and back on one page)
• Convert images to PDF
• Scan and create QR codes

CONVERT
• Convert PDF to DOCX documents, XLSX spreadsheets and PPTX presentations
• Convert DOCX, XLSX, PPTX and text to PDF

DOCUMENTS, SPREADSHEETS, PRESENTATIONS
• Create and edit text documents (DOCX, TXT, Markdown)
• Spreadsheets with formulas (XLSX, CSV)
• Simple presentations (PPTX)
• Templates for CVs, letters, invoices, budgets and more
• Letter generators for Germany (cancellation, withdrawal, objection – the letters are written in German)

AI ASSISTANT (OPTIONAL)
• Chat with a document: ask questions, get answers with clickable page references
• Summarize and translate (save as DOCX, text or PDF)
• Writing help in the editor, formula assistant in spreadsheets, slides by AI
• Flashcards & quizzes from your notes, letters by AI, translate photos
• Free requests every day without an account – more via a voluntary video ad or FaLe Plus
• Only with your consent; only the text you select is sent

HANDY
• "Open with" and "Share": take files straight from email, messengers or your file manager
• Saved files go to the "Documents/FaLe PDF" folder
• Reminders for deadlines and appointments
• Works completely offline

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your files never leave your device. No user account, no banner ads, no analytics. Only when you use the optional cloud AI is the selected text processed – none of it is stored. The camera is only used for scanning, notifications only for reminders you set yourself.

Free. No watermarks. Optional: FaLe Plus for more AI requests.

Available in English and German.
```
