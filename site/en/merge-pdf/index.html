Frequently asked questions

How many PDFs can I merge?

As many as your device can handle. There is no artificial limit.

Can I leave out single pages?

Yes. With “Organize pages” you delete, rotate or reorder pages before or after merging.

Does the text stay selectable?

Yes. The pages are taken over unchanged, so text stays selectable and searchable.
