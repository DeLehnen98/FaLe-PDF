How it works

  1. Open the PDF Open the PDF in FaLe PDF and choose “Fill & Sign”.
  2. Draw your signature Tap “Signature” and draw it once. FaLe remembers it for next time.
  3. Place and save Tap the line, adjust it with “Larger” or “Smaller” and tap “Save”.

Frequently asked questions

Is the signature legally valid?

You add a simple electronic signature – like on paper, only digital. That is enough for many everyday documents. Where the law requires a special form (for example a qualified signature), that form applies.

Is my signature stored?

Only on your device, so you can place it with one tap next time. You can delete it at any time.

Does it work on a computer?

Yes. FaLe PDF runs in the browser and can be installed as an app on Windows and Mac. On a computer you draw with the mouse or touchpad.

Can I fill in form fields too?

Yes. If the PDF has form fields, you fill them in the same step and sign afterwards.
