How it works

  1. Take a photo Open the scanner and hold your phone over the page. The edges are detected automatically.
  2. Check it Adjust the corners if needed and choose a filter such as “Enhanced” or “Black & white”.
  3. Save as PDF Tap “Save PDF”. If you like, the name is suggested from the content.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need internet?

No. Only text recognition downloads its language data once the first time; after that everything works offline.

How is the file name chosen?

If you leave the name field empty, FaLe suggests a name from the content after text recognition. You can change it with one tap.

Can I use photos from my gallery?

Yes. Choose “From album / files” and pick existing pictures.

Is there an ID card mode?

Yes. “Scan ID card” puts the front and back at their original size on one A4 page.
