How it works

  1. Open the form Open the PDF and choose “Fill & Sign”. Existing fields are highlighted.
  2. Fill in the fields Tap a field and start typing. For forms without fields, tap “Text” and then the spot on the form.
  3. Sign and save Add the date, check marks and your signature if needed, then tap “Save”.

Frequently asked questions

What if the form has no fields?

Tap “Text” and then the spot on the form. For tick boxes there is “Check mark” with ✓, ✗ or a dot.

Can I change the form later?

When saving, the fields are normally fixed so nobody can change them unnoticed. If you want to change something later, untick the option to fix the fields before saving.

Are my entries uploaded?

No. Filling in happens entirely on your device.

What about forms that only show “Please wait …”?

These are usually special official forms (XFA). Open them with “Fill in official form” in FaLe PDF.
