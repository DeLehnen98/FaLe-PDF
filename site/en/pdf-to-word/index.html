How it works

  1. Open the PDF Open the PDF and choose “PDF to Word”.
  2. Choose how “Editable text” rebuilds paragraphs and headings, “Exact layout” takes over every page exactly as in the original.
  3. Convert Tap “Convert to Word” – the document is in your files and can be edited right away in FaLe.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Word document look exactly like the PDF?

With “Exact layout”, yes – but then the text cannot be edited. “Editable text” keeps the content and structure; the look may differ slightly.

Can I convert only certain pages?

Yes. Enter the pages, for example “1-3, 5”.

Which format do I get?

A Word document in DOCX format that opens in all common word processors.
