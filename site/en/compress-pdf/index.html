How it works

  1. Open the PDF Open the PDF and choose “Compress PDF”.
  2. Choose how “Shrink images” for scans and photos, “Lossless” for text documents or “Strong” for the smallest file.
  3. Compress Tap the button – you see right away how much smaller the file has become.

Frequently asked questions

Does the quality get worse?

“Lossless” doesn't change the look. “Shrink images” only recalculates photos and scans, text stays sharp. “Strong” turns every page into an image.

Does text stay selectable?

With “Lossless” and “Shrink images”, yes. With “Strong” the pages become images.

How small will the file get?

That depends on the content. Scans and photos usually shrink a lot, pure text files less.

Is there a size limit?

No, only the storage of your device.
