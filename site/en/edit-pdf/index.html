How it works

  1. Open the PDF Open the PDF and choose “Edit PDF”.
  2. Make changes Use “Text” to write something new, “Replace” to tap existing text and change it, and “Image” to add a photo or logo.
  3. Save Tap “Save” – the new version is in your files and the original is kept.

Frequently asked questions

Is the old text really removed?

When replacing, the old text is covered. For confidential passages that must be removed from the file for good, use “Redact”.

Can I add images and logos?

Yes, JPG and PNG. Move and resize them with your finger or mouse.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit, editing also works without internet.

Is my original kept?

Yes. A new version is saved; you can replace the original if you want to.
