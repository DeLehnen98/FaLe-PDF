Frequently asked questions

Is there a limit on files or pages?

No. There is no daily limit and no page limit. How large a file can be only depends on your device’s memory.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Your files are processed and stored on your device. Only when you use the optional cloud AI is the text you select sent to the AI service – with your consent.

Can I edit and sign PDFs without an account?

Yes. Changing text, filling in forms, drawing and placing your signature, dates and stamps – all without an account.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All tools except the optional cloud AI work without internet – including text recognition. Its language data is downloaded once on first use.

Which files can I open?

PDF, Word (DOCX), Excel (XLSX), PowerPoint (PPTX), CSV, TXT, Markdown and images (JPG, PNG, WebP).

Does FaLe PDF run on Windows, Mac, Android and iPhone?

Yes. FaLe PDF runs in any current browser and can be installed like an app on all of these devices. An Android app for the Play Store is coming soon.

Are there ads?

No banners and no ads while you work. In the Android app you can choose to watch a video ad to get extra AI requests.
