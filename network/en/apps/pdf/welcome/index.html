FaLe PDF

FaLe PDF is set up

Good to have you here. Three steps and everything runs by itself.

First steps

  1. Open a file

    Drag a file into the FaLe PDF window – or click “Open file” in the program. This works with PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and pictures.

  2. Use FaLe PDF for every PDF

    Right-click a PDF file, choose “Open with” → “Choose another app” → FaLe PDF and then “Always”. From now on a double click opens every PDF in FaLe PDF. (Windows 10: tick “Always use this app”.)

  3. Fill in and sign

    Open a form and choose “Fill & sign”. You draw your signature once – FaLe PDF remembers it on this PC.

Your files stay on your PC. Only what you start yourself goes over the internet – for example when you share a file.

FaLe PDF for your phone

Scan, sign and send on the go – just as easy to use.

The app will be on the Play Store shortly. Until then FaLe PDF runs in your phone’s browser: just open pdf.fale.network on your phone.
