What applies everywhere here

For the tools, the templates and every FaLe app.

  • No account needed

    Open the app and get started. No sign-up, no email address, no login – not even for features other apps charge a subscription for.

  • Processing on your device

    Files, scans and settings stay on your phone or PC. Whatever works without a server runs without one – often offline, too.

  • End-to-end encrypted once it goes online

    Sharing, connecting devices and, soon, sync are encrypted on your device. All we ever receive is data we cannot read.

  • Fair funding

    No banner ads, no tracking, no selling of data. Real costs, mainly for the cloud AI, are covered by optional reward videos for extra AI requests and the optional FaLe Plus.

Frequently asked questions

What is FaleNetwork?

The common home of all apps by FaLe.dev: one website, one look, the same privacy rules and, soon, an optional shared account.

Why are the apps free?

Because almost everything happens on your device – and that costs us nothing. Running costs come mainly from the cloud AI. Optional reward videos and the optional FaLe Plus cover them.

Do I need an account?

No. Every app works fully without an account. The FaLe Account will be an optional extra for syncing between devices and for FaLe Plus in every app.

Are there ads?

No banners, no pop-ups, no tracking. You only see an ad if you start a video yourself to get extra AI requests.

Are my files uploaded?

Not on their own. Files only leave your device when you deliberately share them or use a cloud AI feature – and then only what that feature needs.

Who is behind this?

FaLe.dev, an independent developer from Saarland, Germany. Full provider details are in the legal notice.
