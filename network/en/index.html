Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Almost everything runs on your device, which costs us nothing. Only the optional cloud AI has real costs – there is a number of free requests every day.

Do I have to install anything or sign up?

No. Everything runs right in the browser, without signing up. You only need a FaLe Account to sync files between your devices.

Are my files uploaded?

Not on their own. Files only leave your device when you deliberately share them or use a cloud feature – end-to-end encrypted wherever possible.

Who is behind this?
