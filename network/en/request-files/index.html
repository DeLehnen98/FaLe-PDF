Request documents – via a link

You send a link, the other person uploads their files. Encrypted on their device – only you can open them.

Runs in FaLe PDF in your browser. Nothing to install, no account – not even for the person uploading.

How it works

  1. Create a request

    Say what you need the documents for and list what is missing – for example a payslip summary, a bank statement or a copy of an ID.

  2. Send the link

    Via messenger, email or QR code. The other person sees your checklist, picks files or takes photos of papers and uploads them.

  3. Open the files

    In FaLe PDF you see what has arrived and what is still missing. Save files one by one, add them to your files or download everything as a ZIP.

What you get

  • Only you can open the filesEvery file is encrypted on the other person’s device – with a key only your device has. Even someone with the link cannot download anything.
  • No account, no appNeither you nor the other person need to sign up. The link opens in any current browser.
  • A checklist instead of follow-upsEach file can be assigned to an item. You see at a glance what is there and what is missing.
  • Deletes itselfYou choose 7, 14 or 30 days. Then the request and its files are deleted from the server automatically – or earlier if you like.

Frequently asked questions

What is it good for?

Whenever you need papers from others: documents for a tax return, job applications, tenant applications, customer data for a job or photos from a family party.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Requesting documents is free and without ads.

How much can be uploaded?

Up to 50 MB per file and up to 50 files with 250 MB in total per request.

Who can see the files?

Only you, on the device where you created the request. Our server only stores encrypted data and sees neither contents nor file names.

Can I stop uploads?

Yes. You can pause new uploads at any time, delete single files or end the whole request.

Does the other person need an app?

No. They open the link in a browser on a phone or computer and can pick files or take a photo directly.
