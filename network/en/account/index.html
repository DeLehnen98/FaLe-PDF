FaLe AccountComing soon

One account for every FaLe app.

The FaLe Account is an optional extra: it keeps your devices in sync and lets you use FaLe Plus in every app – end-to-end encrypted.

Sign in

Sign-in opens as soon as the account is ready. Until then, there’s nothing you need to do.

What the account does

  • One login for every app. Sign in once – in FaLe PDF, FaLe Clean and every future FaLe app.
  • FaLe Plus everywhere. One subscription works in every app. No separate purchase per app.
  • Sync. Keep your data in sync between phone, tablet and PC, if you want to.
  • End-to-end encrypted. Encryption happens on your device before anything is uploaded. All we store is data we cannot read.

What doesn’t change

  • No obligation. Every app works fully without an account – today and in the future.
  • No tracking. The account exists for sync and FaLe Plus, not for building ad profiles.
  • Local first. Even with an account, your device is where the work happens. The cloud is only the encrypted sync.

End-to-end also means: no master key

Because only you can decrypt your data, we can’t recover it for you either. How to secure your access and restore it in an emergency will be explained here when the account launches.

FaLe Plus

FaLe Plus is an optional subscription that supports development and unlocks more cloud AI requests. It is already built into FaLe PDF. With the FaLe Account it will work in every app. All core features stay free.

Questions about the account

When does the FaLe Account launch?

We’re working on it. We’ll announce the launch under News on the home page.

What does the account cost?

The account itself is free. Only the optional FaLe Plus is paid.

Do I have to upload my data?

No. Sync is optional. Without it, everything stays on your device only.
