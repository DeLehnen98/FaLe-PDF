Support

Help keep it free

All tools on fale.network are free – no ads, no account, no tracking. To keep it that way, we appreciate any support, with money or without.

With a contribution

Once or monthly, whatever suits you. Every amount helps – small ones too.

Contributions are being set up right now. Until then, telling others about the tools helps us most.

Help without money

  • Tell others

    Tell friends, family or colleagues about the tools. Your recommendation does more than any ad.

  • Come back

    Bookmark fale.network or install it as an app from your browser menu. Many tools then even work offline.

    Go to the tools

What your contribution pays for

  • Servers, domains and delivering the site across Europe
  • New tools and templates – chosen by what annoys people most elsewhere
  • Testing on many devices so nothing hangs, freezes or damages files
  • Later: independent security reviews

What doesn’t change

  • All tools stay free for everyone – with or without a contribution
  • No ads, no tracking, no cookies
  • Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded

Frequently asked questions

Is this a donation?

Not in the tax sense. FaleNetwork is run by FaLe.dev, an independent developer, not a charity, so there is no donation receipt. Your contribution is voluntary.

Do I get anything for it?

You help keep everything free and add new tools. There are no supporter-only extras – everyone should be able to use every tool.

What happens to my data?

Payments are handled by a payment provider, whose privacy policy applies. We only receive what the provider passes on to process the payment, such as name and amount, and use it for nothing else. This page itself embeds nothing from the provider – a connection is only made when you tap the button.
