Frequently asked questions

Can I change the design later?

Yes. In the editor you switch the design under “1. Design” and all your details stay. Try a few – the preview updates at once.

What does ATS-friendly mean?

Many employers let software read applications first (an applicant tracking system). Such systems read one-column CVs with clear headings and real text most reliably, so the “ATS-friendly” design leaves out columns, graphics, bars and the photo.

How long should a CV be?

One or two pages is usual. The editor flows longer content onto more pages and numbers them. If it gets longer, the “Compact” or “Minimal” design helps – or shorten older positions.

Is my data stored?

No. The CV is created in your browser. Nothing is stored unless you switch on “Save the draft” in the editor – and then only on your device.
