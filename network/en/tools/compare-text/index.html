Compare two texts

Paste two versions and see at once what was deleted, added or changed.

Want to compare two PDF files? Compare PDF

  • Stays on your device
  • No limits
  • No watermark
  • No account
How can I check that nothing is uploaded?

Your files are processed in this browser tab. This page has no function that sends files to a server, and its security rules (Content Security Policy) forbid connections to other servers.

Check it yourself: load this page, then switch on flight mode or disconnect Wi-Fi and run the tool. It still works – because everything happens on your device.

Good to know

How it works – step by step

  1. Paste the texts

    Paste the old version on the left and the new one on the right – from emails, contracts or websites.

  2. Choose how

    Compare by words, characters or lines. Case and spacing can be ignored.

  3. See the differences

    Deleted text is struck through, new text underlined – inline or side by side, with counts.

Why FaLe?

  • No upload

    Both texts stay on your device – important for contracts, applications and internal documents.

  • Words, characters or lines

    By words for prose, by characters for typos, by lines for lists and code.

  • PDFs too

    For two PDF versions there is “Compare PDF” with a page view.

Frequently asked questions
How do I compare two texts?

Paste the first version into the left box and the second into the right one. Differences are highlighted at once.

Are my texts uploaded?

No. The comparison runs in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.

Can I ignore spaces and case?

Yes. Tick the options and only real content changes are counted.

How do I compare two PDF files?

Use “Compare PDF”: it reads the text of both PDFs and marks the changes on the pages.
