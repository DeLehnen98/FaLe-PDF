Frequently asked questions
How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Divide the number by 100 and multiply by the percentage: 19 % of 250 = 250 ÷ 100 × 19 = 47.5.

What percentage is one number of another?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100: 45 of 180 = 45 ÷ 180 × 100 = 25 %.

How do I calculate a percentage change?

New value minus old value, divided by the old value, times 100. From 80 to 92 is (92 − 80) ÷ 80 × 100 = +15 %.

What is the difference between percent and percentage points?

If a rate rises from 10 % to 12 %, that is 2 percentage points – but an increase of 20 %.
