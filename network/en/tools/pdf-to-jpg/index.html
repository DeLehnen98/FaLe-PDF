How can I check that nothing is uploaded?

Your files are processed in this browser tab. This page has no function that sends files to a server, and its security rules (Content Security Policy) forbid connections to other servers.

Check it yourself: load this page, then switch on flight mode or disconnect Wi-Fi and run the tool. It still works – because everything happens on your device.

How it works – step by step

  1. Choose a PDF

    Pick one or more PDFs. Unlock protected PDFs right on the file card with the password.

  2. Pick pages and quality

    Enter pages if you like (e.g. 1-3, 5) and set the resolution and JPG quality. The bar at the bottom shows roughly how large the images will be.

    One page comes straight as an image, several pages one by one or as a ZIP. On your phone, share them with one tap, for example to a messenger.

Frequently asked questions
Which resolution should I choose?

For messengers and screens, 100 to 150 dpi is enough. For printing use 300 dpi. 600 dpi only pays off for very small details; the files get large.

Can I save just one page as an image?

Yes. Enter the pages you want, for example “2” or “1-3, 7”. Empty means all pages.

Do I get a ZIP file?

Only if you want one. A single page downloads directly as a JPG. With several pages you can save each image or all of them as a ZIP.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser. After the first visit the tool also works offline.

JPG or PNG – which is better?

JPG is smaller and ideal for photos and scans. PNG is lossless, razor-sharp for text and graphics and can have a transparent background – use “PDF to PNG” for that.
