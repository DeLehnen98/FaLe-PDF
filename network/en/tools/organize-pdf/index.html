Organize PDF pages

Press and hold a page, then drag it to its new place.

  • Stays on your device
  • No limits
  • No watermark
  • No account
How can I check that nothing is uploaded?

Your files are processed in this browser tab. This page has no function that sends files to a server, and its security rules (Content Security Policy) forbid connections to other servers.

Check it yourself: load this page, then switch on flight mode or disconnect Wi-Fi and run the tool. It still works – because everything happens on your device.

How it works

  1. Choose a PDF

    Pick a PDF. You can also choose several at once – their pages can then be mixed freely.

  2. Arrange the pages

    Press and hold a page and drag it to its new place. Rotate, delete, duplicate or move selected pages with the bar on top. Made a mistake? Just undo.

  3. Save

    Tap “Save” and download the reordered PDF or share it.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and your PDFs never leave your device. After the first visit it works offline, too.

How do I move a page on a phone?

Press and hold the page until it lifts, then drag it to its new place. The view scrolls along at the edge. You can also select pages and move them forward or back with the arrows.

Can I move several pages at once?

Yes. Select the pages and drag one of them – all selected pages move to the new place together.

How do I insert pages from another PDF?

Select the page after which the new pages should go and tap “Insert PDF”. The pages of the chosen file show up there and can be moved like all others.

What size is an inserted blank page?

It gets the size of the page before it, for example A4 portrait or landscape, so it fits the rest of the document.

Are bookmarks and forms kept?

Yes, bookmarks, links and form fields of the first PDF are kept. Whatever belonged to deleted pages is removed with them.
