Rotate PDF pages

Rotate the whole PDF or single pages – the preview shows the result right away.

  • Stays on your device
  • No limits
  • No watermark
  • No account
How can I check that nothing is uploaded?

Your files are processed in this browser tab. This page has no function that sends files to a server, and its security rules (Content Security Policy) forbid connections to other servers.

Check it yourself: load this page, then switch on flight mode or disconnect Wi-Fi and run the tool. It still works – because everything happens on your device.

How it works

  1. Choose a PDF

    Pick your PDF. Every page shows up as a thumbnail.

  2. Rotate

    The arrows on top rotate all pages – or only the ones you tapped. The round button on a page turns just that page. “Landscape” or “Portrait” selects all pages of one format at once.

  3. Save

    Tap “Save”. The pages stay rotated in every app.

Why FaLe?

  • See it instantly

    Every rotation shows up in the preview right away, before you save.

  • One or all

    A button on each page for quick fixes, the bar on top for all or selected pages.

  • Select by format

    Select all landscape or portrait pages with one tap – handy for mixed scans.

  • Lossless

    Only the orientation of the page changes. Text, images and quality stay exactly the same.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. The PDF is rotated in your browser and never leaves your device.

Is the rotation permanent?

Yes. The rotation is saved in the PDF, so it stays in every viewer and when printing.

Does rotating reduce the quality?

No. Only the orientation of the pages changes, content and images stay untouched. The file keeps practically the same size.

How do I rotate only some pages?

Tap the round rotate button on a page to turn just that one. Or tap several pages and then use the arrows on top – they only affect the selection.

Is password protection kept?

Yes. If you open a protected PDF with its password, the rotated PDF is protected again with the same password.
