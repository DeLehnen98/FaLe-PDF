Convert PDF to Word

Turn a PDF into a Word document (DOCX) you can keep writing in.

  • Stays on your device
  • No limits
  • No watermark
  • No account
How can I check that nothing is uploaded?

Your files are processed in this browser tab. This page has no function that sends files to a server, and its security rules (Content Security Policy) forbid connections to other servers.

Check it yourself: load this page, then switch on flight mode or disconnect Wi-Fi and run the tool. It still works – because everything happens on your device.

How it works

  1. Choose a PDF

    Pick a PDF with real text, for example a letter, a report or a contract.

  2. Choose the type

    “Editable” keeps text, headings, lists, tables and images. “Exact look” places every page as a picture – looks exactly the same but can’t be edited.

  3. Save the Word file

    Check the detected structure in the preview, then download the DOCX file or share it straight from your phone.

Why FaLe?

  • Structure, not a wall of text

    Headings become Word headings, bullet points real lists and aligned columns a table.

  • Bold, italic and images

    Emphasis, font sizes and embedded images come along in the right place.

  • Two columns, right order

    Two-column pages are taken over column by column, repeating page numbers are dropped.

  • No upload

    Contracts and letters stay on your device. The conversion runs entirely in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Word document look exactly like the PDF?

With “Editable” the layout is simplified: text flows in paragraphs so you can edit it. Simple documents come across very well, complex layouts with text boxes or overlapping elements not always. If you need the exact look, choose “Exact look”.

What about scanned PDFs?

Scans contain no text, only images. Such pages are inserted as pictures. To edit their text you need text recognition (OCR) – the FaLe PDF app does that offline on your device.

Are tables kept?

Yes, when the columns in the PDF are cleanly aligned. Merged cells and deeply nested tables may come out simplified.

Which programs open the file?

Any program that reads DOCX files – on a computer, in the browser or in office apps on your phone.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The PDF is read in your browser and the Word document is created there.
