Annotate and highlight a PDF

Highlight, comment and draw right in the PDF – every PDF program shows your notes afterwards.

  • Stays on your device
  • No limits
  • No watermark
  • No account
How can I check that nothing is uploaded?

Your files are processed in this browser tab. This page has no function that sends files to a server, and its security rules (Content Security Policy) forbid connections to other servers.

Check it yourself: load this page, then switch on flight mode or disconnect Wi-Fi and run the tool. It still works – because everything happens on your device.

Good to know

How it works – step by step

  1. Choose a PDF

    The first page appears right away – use the arrows to turn pages.

  2. Pick a tool and comment

    Highlight, underline, strike out, note, text, draw, rectangle or arrow. Undo any time.

  3. Save

    You get the PDF with real comments – and, if you like, all highlights as a text summary.

Why FaLe?

  • Finger, pen or mouse

  • No upload, no daily limit

Frequently asked questions
Why can’t I highlight text in some PDFs?

Scanned PDFs contain only pictures. Make them searchable with “OCR PDF” first – drawing, notes and rectangles work without it.

Does the recipient see my comments?

Yes. The comments are real PDF annotations and are shown in all common PDF programs – including your comment text.

Can I change comments later?

In any PDF program that can edit comments. Here you can undo everything or delete single comments before saving.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser. After the first visit the page also works offline.
