How can I check that nothing is uploaded?

Your files are processed in this browser tab. This page has no function that sends files to a server, and its security rules (Content Security Policy) forbid connections to other servers.

Check it yourself: load this page, then switch on flight mode or disconnect Wi-Fi and run the tool. It still works – because everything happens on your device.

How it works

  1. Enter the amount

    The amount in words appears right away. Show the included tax if you need it.

  2. What for and from whom

    Add the purpose, payer, payment method and yourself as recipient. Empty fields become lines to fill in by hand.

  3. Print

    Choose one, two or three receipts per A4 page – for example an original and a copy – and create the PDF.

Frequently asked questions

What should a receipt contain?

Usually the amount in figures and words, the payer, the purpose, the date and place, and the recipient’s signature. A number helps with filing.

Can I print blank receipts?

Yes. Leave the amount and other fields empty and choose three per page – empty fields are printed as lines.

Which currencies are supported?

Euro, pound sterling, US dollar and Swiss franc, including the amount in words.

Is my data stored?

No. Only if you switch it on does your browser remember your recipient details.
