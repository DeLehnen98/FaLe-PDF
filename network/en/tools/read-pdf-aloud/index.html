Read a PDF aloud

Sit back: your browser reads the PDF aloud – and highlights where you are.

  • Stays on your device
  • No limits
  • No watermark
  • No account
How can I check that nothing is uploaded?

Your files are processed in this browser tab. This page has no function that sends files to a server, and its security rules (Content Security Policy) forbid connections to other servers.

Check it yourself: load this page, then switch on flight mode or disconnect Wi-Fi and run the tool. It still works – because everything happens on your device.

Good to know

How it works – step by step

  1. Choose a PDF

    Or a text file. Page numbers, headers and footers are filtered out.

  2. Choose voice and speed

    Your device’s voices are used – changeable while reading.

  3. Listen

    The current sentence is highlighted. Tap a paragraph to continue there.

Why FaLe?

  • No listening limit, no subscription

    Other services limit the minutes or want a subscription for better voices. Here your device reads as long as you like.

  • Reads what matters

    No page numbers and headers in between, hyphenated words joined, columns in the right order.

  • Scanned PDFs too

    Pages without text can be made readable with text recognition – right in the browser.

  • Remembers your place

    Come back later and it continues where you stopped.

Frequently asked questions
Why do the voices sound different on each device?

Reading uses your device’s or browser’s speech output. On many phones and computers you can install better voices for free in the system settings.

Can I start in the middle?

Yes. Tap a sentence or paragraph – reading jumps there. The arrows move paragraph by paragraph.

Are the bibliography and tables read?

The bibliography and tables are left out by default. Both can be switched on in the settings.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Text and speech run on your device. Note: some browsers also offer online voices (marked ☁) – those send the text to the provider. Choose a voice without ☁ if that should not happen.
