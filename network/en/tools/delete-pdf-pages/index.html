Delete pages from a PDF

Tap the pages you want gone – everything else stays as it is.

  • Stays on your device
  • No limits
  • No watermark
  • No account
How can I check that nothing is uploaded?

Your files are processed in this browser tab. This page has no function that sends files to a server, and its security rules (Content Security Policy) forbid connections to other servers.

Check it yourself: load this page, then switch on flight mode or disconnect Wi-Fi and run the tool. It still works – because everything happens on your device.

Good to know

How it works – step by step

  1. Choose a PDF

    Pick your PDF. Every page shows up as a thumbnail.

  2. Mark pages

    Tap the pages you want to get rid of – they turn red. Or type ranges such as “2, 5-7”. You always see how many pages are left.

  3. Delete and save

    Tap “Delete pages” and download the new PDF or share it right away.

Why FaLe?

  • The rest stays untouched

  • Quick marking

Frequently asked questions
Are my files uploaded?

No. The PDF is edited entirely in your browser and never leaves your device. It even works in flight mode once the page has loaded.

Are deleted pages really gone from the file?

Yes. The content of deleted pages is removed from the file, as are bookmarks, link targets and form fields that only belonged to those pages. Images or fonts that are also used on other pages are kept, of course.

How do I type page ranges?

Separate single pages with commas and write ranges with a dash: “2, 5-7, 10-” deletes page 2, pages 5 to 7 and everything from page 10. The preview shows the marked pages instantly.

Is password protection kept?

Yes. If you open a protected PDF with its password, the result is protected again with the same password.

Can blank pages be found automatically?

Yes, use the “Remove blank pages” tool. It detects blank and almost blank pages, scans included, and shows them to you for a check before anything is deleted.
