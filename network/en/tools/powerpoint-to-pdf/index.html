Convert PowerPoint to PDF

Turn a presentation (PowerPoint, PPTX) into a PDF to send or use as a handout.

  • Stays on your device
  • No limits
  • No watermark
  • No account
How can I check that nothing is uploaded?

Your files are processed in this browser tab. This page has no function that sends files to a server, and its security rules (Content Security Policy) forbid connections to other servers.

Check it yourself: load this page, then switch on flight mode or disconnect Wi-Fi and run the tool. It still works – because everything happens on your device.

Good to know

How it works – step by step

  1. Choose a presentation

    Pick one or more PPTX files.

  2. Convert

    Tap “Convert to PDF”. Every slide becomes a page in the slide format.

  3. Check and share

    Swipe through the preview, then download the PDF or share it right away.

Frequently asked questions
Does the PDF look exactly like my presentation?

Texts, images, shapes, tables and backgrounds are redrawn. Special effects (shadows, 3D, word art), charts, embedded videos and animations are simplified or missing. So take a quick look at the preview.

Are fonts kept?

The slides are drawn with your device’s fonts. If a font is missing, a similar one is used – line breaks may shift slightly.

Do old .ppt files work?

No, only PPTX. Save a .ppt file as PPTX in your presentation app.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is read in your browser and the PDF is created there. After the first visit it even works offline.
