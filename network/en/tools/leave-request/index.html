Create a leave request

Pick your dates – the tool counts the working days for you.

  • Stays on your device
  • No limits
  • No watermark
  • No account
How can I check that nothing is uploaded?

Your files are processed in this browser tab. This page has no function that sends files to a server, and its security rules (Content Security Policy) forbid connections to other servers.

Check it yourself: load this page, then switch on flight mode or disconnect Wi-Fi and run the tool. It still works – because everything happens on your device.

Good to know

How it works – step by step

  1. Choose the dates

    Enter one or more periods. Weekends are left out; if you work in Germany, pick your state to leave out its public holidays too.

  2. Add your details

    Name, department, type of leave, stand-in and, if you like, your annual entitlement to see what is left.

  3. Sign and hand in

    Sign with your finger or on the printout. There is a box with signature lines for the approval.

Why FaLe?

  • Working days counted

    Weekends, optional German public holidays per state, 5- or 6-day week and half days on 24 and 31 December.

  • Remaining leave at a glance

    Entitlement, days already taken and this request give you the remaining days right away.

  • Ready for HR

    Fields for approval, manager and HR – neatly on one A4 page.

  • Stays with you

    Everything is created in your browser and only stored if you want.

Frequently asked questions
Which public holidays are counted?

None by default. You can choose a German state to leave out its statewide public holidays; other countries are not included.

I also work on Saturdays – what now?

Choose the 6-day week, then Monday to Saturday count as working days.

Is the request approved by this?

No. Your employer approves it – there is a box for that at the bottom of the page.

Is my data stored?

Only if you switch on “Remember my details” – then in this browser. Switching it off deletes them.
