Create a fillable PDF form

Pick a PDF, place the fields – and you have a form anyone can fill in on a computer or phone.

  • Stays on your device
  • No limits
  • No watermark
  • No account
How can I check that nothing is uploaded?

Your files are processed in this browser tab. This page has no function that sends files to a server, and its security rules (Content Security Policy) forbid connections to other servers.

Check it yourself: load this page, then switch on flight mode or disconnect Wi-Fi and run the tool. It still works – because everything happens on your device.

Good to know

How it works – step by step

  1. Choose a PDF

    Take a finished template, an application or a scanned form as a PDF.

  2. Place fields

    Tap a field type and drag the field into place – or let lines, boxes and “Name: ____” be detected automatically.

  3. Save the form

    You get a PDF with real form fields that can be filled in with any PDF app.

Why FaLe?

  • Usually part of a subscription

    Many PDF programs charge extra for creating fillable forms. Here it is free – no account, no watermark.

  • All the important field types

    Text field, multi-line text, check box, option group, drop-down, date with format check and signature field – with required flag, prefilled value and help text.

  • Detect fields automatically

    Underlines, “Label:” gaps, drawn boxes and lines are found and suggested as fields. You only check them.

  • No upload

    Your PDF is edited in your browser and never uploaded – even confidential applications and contracts.

Frequently asked questions
What can fill in the form?

Any common PDF program on a computer, PDF apps on phones and browsers – also here with “Fill PDF form”.

Are fields the PDF already has kept?

Yes. Existing form fields stay unchanged; new fields with the same name are numbered automatically.

In which order does the Tab key move?

Row by row from top to bottom, and left to right within a row – the way you read. The list “All fields in filling order” shows it beforehand. Made a mistake? “Undo” (or Ctrl+Z) takes back every step.

What is a signature field?

A marked area where the person filling in can sign in their PDF app. It stays empty until someone signs.

Does it work with scanned forms?

Yes. Just place the fields on the image of the page. Automatic detection finds less on scans because they contain no real lines.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser; after the first visit the page also works offline.
