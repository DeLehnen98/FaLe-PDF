Create an operating cost statement

Enter costs, tenants and prepayments – each tenant gets a clear statement as a letter.

  • Stays on your device
  • No limits

How can I check that nothing is uploaded?

Your files are processed in this browser tab. This page has no function that sends files to a server, and its security rules (Content Security Policy) forbid connections to other servers.

Check it yourself: load this page, then switch on flight mode or disconnect Wi-Fi and run the tool. It still works – because everything happens on your device.

Good to know

How it works – step by step

  1. Period and house

    Choose the billing period (at most 12 months) and enter the total living area and number of flats – including empty and owner-occupied ones.

  2. Costs and tenants

    Enter the operating costs with their allocation key, and the tenants with area, persons and prepayments. For a change of tenant the move dates count.

  3. Create statements

    Check the preview per tenant and create the letters as PDF – together or one each as a ZIP, plus an overview as CSV.

Why FaLe?

  • No yearly subscription

    Landlord software often charges yearly or monthly. For a few flats your browser is enough – free.

  • Formally sound

    Each statement shows total costs, the allocation key explained, the tenant’s share and the prepayments deducted – as German case law requires.

  • Tenant changes and vacancy

    Costs are split by the day. Whatever falls on empty or owner-occupied flats automatically stays with you.

  • Your data stays with you

    Names, amounts and flats are not uploaded. Kept for next year only if you switch that on.

Frequently asked questions
What must the statement contain?

The billing period, total costs per cost type, the allocation key explained, the tenant’s share and the deduction of their prepayments. Every statement here is built that way.

When must tenants receive it?

At the latest twelve months after the end of the billing period (§ 556 (3) BGB). After that no back payment can be claimed. FaLe shows you the deadline.

Which allocation key do I use?

The one agreed in the lease. Without an agreement, living area applies; costs recorded by meters, such as water, go by consumption (§ 556a BGB).

How do I handle heating costs?

German rules require heating and hot water to be split mainly by consumption, usually by a metering company. Enter its amounts with the key “Amount per tenant”.

Is this legal advice?

No. FaLe calculates and lays out the statement but doesn’t check your lease.
