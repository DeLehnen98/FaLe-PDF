How can I check that nothing is uploaded?

Your files are processed in this browser tab. This page has no function that sends files to a server, and its security rules (Content Security Policy) forbid connections to other servers.

Check it yourself: load this page, then switch on flight mode or disconnect Wi-Fi and run the tool. It still works – because everything happens on your device.

Good to know

How it works – step by step

  1. Choose photos

    Pick one or many photos – JPG, PNG, WebP or from an iPhone. They all get the same watermark.

  2. Set text and look

    Type your name or website, choose a corner or “all over the photo”, size, colour and opacity. The preview updates right away.

  3. Save

    Tap “Add watermark”. Download several photos together or share them directly.

Why FaLe?

  • No third-party logo

    Many apps add their own logo or want a subscription to export. Here only your text goes on the picture.

  • Same on every photo

    The size follows the shorter side. Portrait or landscape, large or small – the watermark looks the same everywhere.

  • Hard to remove

    Spread across the whole photo, the watermark can’t just be cropped away. A smaller copy for the web protects even more.

  • Photos stay with you

    Everything runs in your browser. Location and camera data are removed when saving.

Frequently asked questions
Can I watermark several photos at once?

Yes. Pick as many photos as you like – they all get the same watermark at a fitting size.

Where should the watermark go?

For listings and portfolios, “all over the photo” at low opacity works best – it can’t be cropped away. For photos that should look nice, a corner is enough.

Does the photo lose quality?

It is re-saved at high quality; you practically can’t see a difference. PNG stays lossless.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The watermark is added in your browser; the photos never leave your device.

Can I watermark a PDF or an ID copy?

For PDFs use “Watermark PDF”. For ID copies use “Copy ID card” – it can note who the copy is for.
