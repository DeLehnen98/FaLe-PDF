We use no cookies, no tracking pixels and no analytics services, and we embed no third-party content (such as fonts, videos or maps).

Only if you switch between the light and dark theme with the button at the top right does your browser store that choice locally under the name “fale-theme”. The legal basis is § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, as the storage is strictly necessary for the function you requested.
