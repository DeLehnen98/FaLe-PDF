2. Welche Daten verarbeitet die App?

FaLe PDF ist ein Werkzeug zum Lesen, Bearbeiten und Erstellen von Dateien. Alle Inhalte, die du öffnest, erstellst oder bearbeitest (z. B. PDFs, Word-, Excel- und PowerPoint-Dateien, Text, Bilder, Scans, Unterschriften, Notizen, Lesezeichen und Einstellungen), werden nur im Speicher deines Geräts abgelegt – in der Android-App im privaten App-Speicher, in der Web-Version im Speicher deines Browsers (IndexedDB/localStorage). Der Anbieter hat darauf keinen Zugriff.

Diese Daten bleiben gespeichert, bis du sie in der App löschst, die App deinstallierst bzw. die Website-Daten in deinem Browser löschst.

3. Berechtigungen der Android-App

  • Kamera – nur, wenn du die Scan-Funktion nutzt, um Dokumente zu fotografieren. Die Bilder werden lokal zu einer PDF verarbeitet und nicht übertragen. Die Berechtigung wird erst bei der ersten Nutzung abgefragt und kann jederzeit in den Android-Einstellungen entzogen werden.
  • Benachrichtigungen – nur für Erinnerungen, die du selbst in der App einstellst. Die Erinnerungen werden lokal auf dem Gerät geplant.
  • Speicher (nur Android 10 und älter) – um Dateien, die du ausdrücklich speicherst, im Ordner „Dokumente/FaLe PDF“ abzulegen. Auf neueren Android-Versionen ist dafür keine Berechtigung nötig.
  • Vibration – für kurze haptische Rückmeldungen.
  • Internet – wird nur für die optionale Cloud-KI (Abschnitt 7), freiwillige Werbevideos (Abschnitt 8) und Käufe über Google Play (Abschnitt 9) genutzt. Alle anderen Funktionen senden keine Daten.
  • Werbe-ID (AD_ID, nur Android-App) – wird vom Google-Mobile-Ads-SDK nur verwendet, wenn du ein Werbevideo ansiehst (Abschnitt 8). Du kannst sie in den Android-Einstellungen zurücksetzen oder löschen.
  • In-App-Käufe (Google Play Billing) – nur, wenn du freiwillig „FaLe Plus“ abschließt (Abschnitt 9).

Wenn du eine Datei aus einer anderen App mit FaLe PDF öffnest oder an FaLe PDF teilst, wird sie lokal in die App übernommen. Wenn du eine Datei aus FaLe PDF teilst oder speicherst, entscheidest du selbst über das Ziel (z. B. eine Messenger-, E-Mail- oder Cloud-App). Wenn du in Android die automatische Sicherung („Google-Sicherung“) aktiviert hast, kann Android App-Daten in deinem eigenen Google-Konto sichern, damit sie auf einem neuen Gerät wiederhergestellt werden können. Diese Sicherung steuerst du in den Android-Einstellungen; der Anbieter von FaLe PDF hat keinen Zugriff darauf.

2. What data does the app process?

FaLe PDF is a tool for reading, editing and creating files. All content you open, create or edit (e.g. PDFs, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, text, images, scans, signatures, notes, bookmarks and settings) is stored only in your device’s storage – in the Android app in the private app storage, in the web version in your browser’s storage (IndexedDB/localStorage). The provider has no access to it.

This data remains stored until you delete it in the app, uninstall the app or delete the website data in your browser.

3. Permissions of the Android app

  • Camera – only when you use the scan feature to photograph documents. The images are processed into a PDF locally and are not transferred. The permission is only requested on first use and can be revoked at any time in the Android settings.
  • Notifications – only for reminders you set yourself in the app. The reminders are scheduled locally on the device.
  • Storage (Android 10 and older only) – to save files you explicitly save to the “Documents/FaLe PDF” folder. Newer Android versions don’t need a permission for this.
  • Vibration – for short haptic feedback.
  • Internet – only used for the optional cloud AI (section 7), voluntary video ads (section 8) and purchases via Google Play (section 9). All other features send no data.

  • In-app purchases (Google Play Billing) – only if you voluntarily subscribe to “FaLe Plus” (section 9).

4. Opening, sharing and saving files

When you open a file from another app with FaLe PDF or share it to FaLe PDF, it is imported into the app locally. When you share or save a file from FaLe PDF, you decide on the destination yourself (e.g. a messenger, email or cloud app). The provider of that app is then responsible for further processing.

5. Installation via Google Play and Android backup

When you download the app via Google Play, Google processes data (e.g. your Google account and device information) under its own responsibility. Google’s privacy policy applies. The provider of FaLe PDF only receives anonymous, aggregated statistics from Google (e.g. number of installs, crash reports without file contents), insofar as you have agreed to this in the Android settings.

If you have enabled automatic backup (“Google backup”) in Android, Android may back up app data to your own Google account so that it can be restored on a new device. You control this backup in the Android settings; the provider of FaLe PDF has no access to it.

7. Optional cloud AI (AI assistant)

FaLe PDF offers optional AI features (e.g. chat with a document, summarize, translate, writing help, slides, flashcards, letters, translate a photo). Where your browser supports it, summarizing and translating can also run entirely on your device (“On device”) – then nothing is transferred. For the cloud AI:

  • What is sent? Only the text you select for the task: e.g. your question and the most relevant passages of the document (with page numbers), the selected text in the writing help, your request for a letter or the text recognised on a photo. The document itself, photos and your sender details from “My details” are not sent. Text recognition (OCR) happens on your device.
  • To whom? Via our server (hosting: [HOSTING-ANBIETER, z. B. Vercel Inc., USA]) to the AI provider [KI-ANBIETER, z. B. Anthropic, PBC, 548 Market Street, PMB 90375, San Francisco, CA 94104, USA], which generates the answer.   • What do we store? No content and no logs of your input or the answers. To prevent abuse and for the free daily quota, our database ([Supabase, region Frankfurt (eu-central-1)]) only stores counters: a pseudonymous device identifier (derived from a random identifier created in the app – not linked to your name or an account), your number of requests per day and a salted hash of your IP address (changing daily; the IP address itself is not stored). This data is deleted automatically after 30 days. We also keep a monthly total of AI costs without any personal reference.

Processing the hash and counters to prevent abuse is additionally based on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Please do not send particularly sensitive data (e.g. health data) to the cloud AI if you don’t want to. AI results may contain errors.

8. Voluntary video ads (Android app only)

Only when you tap “Watch a video ad” is the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, loaded. Before that, the app asks for your consent via Google’s consent platform (UMP) on whether personalised ads may be shown. Without consent, only non-personalised ads are loaded where possible.

Google processes, among other things, your device’s advertising ID, device and app information, the IP address and interactions with the ad in order to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. So that we can credit the reward, Google sends our server a signed confirmation with a transaction number and your pseudonymous device identifier (not the advertising ID). The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG); you can change it at any time under “AI settings → Ad consent”. More information: How Google uses information from apps.

The legal basis is the performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The data is deleted 30 days after the subscription ends. FaLe Plus cannot currently be purchased in the web version.
